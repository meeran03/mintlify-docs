## Platform Fees

Every customer pays a simple platform fee based on usage:

| Service | Platform Fee |
|---------|-------------|
| **Voice Calls** | $0.03 per active voice minute |
| **SMS Messages** | $0.001 per segment |

<Info>
The current voice runtime meters active seconds. The platform charge rounds up to the next cent per call; providers and carriers use their own measured usage units. SMS platform billing has a one-cent minimum for each nonzero message.
</Info>

## Burki Cloud Pricing

With supported Burki Cloud providers, you pay the platform fee plus actual AI and telephone usage. Burki adds no management markup:

| Component | How It's Billed |
|-----------|-----------------|
| Platform Fee | $0.03/min (voice), $0.001/segment (SMS) |
| Carrier Costs | Pass-through at cost |
| LLM (OpenAI, Anthropic, etc.) | Pass-through at cost |
| STT (Deepgram, Azure) | Pass-through at cost |
| TTS (Cartesia, ElevenLabs) | Pass-through at cost |
| Phone Numbers | Recurring carrier rental and applicable management charges; review your workspace's quote |

<Accordion title="Example: GPT Live 1 with GPT-5.4 mini, five minutes">
This planning example assumes five active call minutes and five provider-session minutes, with 1,000 uncached input tokens and 200 output tokens per minute. Actual usage, session overhead and per-call rounding change the final charge.

| Component | Calculation | Estimated amount |
|-----------|-------------|------------------|
| Platform | 5 minutes × $0.03 | $0.15 |
| GPT Live 1 voice | 5 session minutes × $0.05 | $0.25 |
| GPT-5.4 mini reasoning | 5 × ($0.00075 input + $0.00090 output) | $0.00825 |
| **Total before telephone costs** | | **About $0.41** |

Telephone usage, number rental/management, optional actions and applicable taxes are additional. GPT Live is a voice-session configuration with separate reasoning usage; this is not a universal STT/LLM/TTS tariff.
</Accordion>

## BYO (Bring Your Own) Pricing

When you bring your own API keys, you only pay platform fees:

| Component | How It's Billed |
|-----------|-----------------|
| Platform Fee | $0.03/min (voice), $0.001/segment (SMS) |
| All AI Providers | You pay directly (not through Burki) |

<Accordion title="Example: 5-minute BYO call">
| Fee Type | Calculation | Amount |
|----------|-------------|--------|
| Platform Fee | 5 min × $0.03 | $0.15 |
| LLM/STT/TTS/Carrier | Paid directly to providers | Separate external bill |
| **Total to Burki** | | **$0.15** |
</Accordion>

## Hybrid Mode

Mix and match! Use your own keys for some services while letting Burki manage others.

<Accordion title="Example: BYO LLM with Burki Cloud Nova-3 and Sonic-3">
Assume five active minutes, five Nova-3 audio minutes and 5,000 billable Sonic-3 characters. Your LLM provider bills you separately.

| Component | Calculation | Estimated amount |
|-----------|-------------|------------------|
| Platform | 5 minutes × $0.03 | $0.15 |
| Nova-3 STT | 5 audio minutes × $0.0077 | $0.0385 |
| Sonic-3 TTS | 5,000 characters × $0.05 / 1,000 | $0.25 |
| **Total to Burki before telephone costs** | | **About $0.44** |
| LLM with your own credentials | Provider's actual usage | Separate external bill |
</Accordion>

<Info>
For each supported Burki Cloud component, actual provider usage is passed through at cost. Components using your own verified credentials are billed directly by those providers. Neither mode adds a Burki management markup. Review the exact rates and telephone route in your workspace before going live.
</Info>

## Browser Trial

Eligible new workspaces receive up to **three minutes of personalized browser testing**, pooled across sessions, with a
**two-minute maximum per call** and one trial call at a time. Provider-cost limits and shared daily capacity can reduce
availability. Your workspace shows the effective allowance before testing.

Trial actions are simulated. The allowance does not include a free telephone number, live telephone calls, real bookings,
stored recordings or separately priced AI workflow generation. Those require funded usage and the relevant supported setup.

<Info>
Existing grants and funded balances keep their own terms. Browser trial time is separate from wallet funds, and exhausting
the allowance does not automatically turn practice into paid usage. See the [Quickstart](/quickstart) for the current setup path.
</Info>

## Wallet & Payments

Burki uses a prepaid wallet system:

- **Add funds** via credit card (Stripe)
- **Auto top-up** available to never run out of credits
- **Real-time balance** visible in your dashboard
- **Detailed usage** breakdown by call, assistant, and service

### Minimum Balance Requirements

Before funded usage, review the selected configuration's quote and available balance in **Usage & billing**. Calls can reserve funds before starting, and provider usage can settle afterward. The workspace's readiness checks determine whether the call can proceed.

## Frequently Asked Questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How is voice call duration calculated?">
    The current voice runtime bills the platform from active seconds, rounded up to the next cent per call. At the standard $0.03/minute rate, 30 active seconds costs $0.02 in platform fees. Provider-session, audio, token, character and carrier usage are measured separately, so their billed durations may differ.
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="What counts as an SMS segment?">
    A standard SMS segment is 160 characters. Messages longer than 160 characters are split into multiple segments. We use the carrier's reported segment count.
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="Can I switch between BYO and Burki Cloud?">
    Yes! You can change your configuration at any time from the dashboard. You can even use hybrid mode with some services on BYO and others on Burki Cloud.
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="What happens when my trial ends?">
    Your draft remains editable. Exhausting browser trial time does not automatically authorize paid calling. Add prepaid funds only when you choose to continue, review optional auto top-up separately, and resolve readiness checks before connecting real callers.
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="Is there a monthly minimum or commitment?">
    No. Burki is pure pay-as-you-go. No monthly minimums, no long-term contracts. Pay only for what you use.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
