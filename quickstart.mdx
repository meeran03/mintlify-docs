title: Quickstart
description: "Create a business assistant draft, practice in your browser, and review it before connecting callers."
---

Start with one real conversation: what will a caller need, what facts can your assistant use, and what should happen next?
You can prepare and practice a draft before purchasing a telephone number or configuring provider keys.

## 1. Create your account

[Sign up for Burki](https://burki.dev/auth/register), verify your email when requested, and open **Assistants**.

Eligible new workspaces receive **up to three minutes of personalized browser testing**, pooled across sessions, with a
**two-minute maximum per call** and one trial call at a time. The available allowance is shown before testing. Daily trial
capacity and provider-cost limits can reduce availability; a trial is not a cash wallet credit or a free telephone number.

<Info>
Existing grants and funded balances keep their own terms. Check your workspace's allowance and billing details for what is available to your account.
</Info>

## 2. Prepare an unpublished draft

<Steps>
  <Step title="Describe your business and the call">
    Choose a starter such as receptionist or appointment booking, then enter the business facts and desired outcome.
    For example: “Northside Plumbing handles residential repairs in Boston. Capture the caller's issue, service address,
    urgency and callback number. Do not quote a price or promise an appointment.”
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose how to prepare it">
    The free starter creates editable instructions from your description. Optional AI workflow generation is a separately
    priced operation that requires funded usage; browser trial time does not cover it. Review its displayed price before starting.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the instructions and voice">
    New assistants start with the managed GPT Live preset. Read the greeting, business facts, requested information and
    limits on promises. GPT Live handles speech directly, so a separate STT and TTS configuration is not required for this preset.
    Advanced provider choices remain available where supported.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## 3. Practice in your browser

Select **Test this version**, review the available allowance, and allow microphone access when requested.
Ask a realistic question, interrupt with a correction, and check how the assistant handles information it does not know.

Trial external actions are simulated and should be labeled that way. Live bookings, telephone calls, IVR exploration and
stored recordings require funded usage and the relevant connections. A trial booking response is not a calendar reservation.

After the call, inspect the transcript and available activity. Edit the draft and practice again with the remaining allowance.
Testing an unpublished draft does not publish it.

<Tip>
If testing is temporarily unavailable or the allowance is exhausted, keep editing your draft. Review the reason shown in
Burki before retrying or choosing prepaid usage; trial testing does not automatically turn into paid usage.
</Tip>

## 4. Connect real callers when ready

1. Review [usage pricing](https://burki.dev/pricing) and add prepaid funds through **Usage & billing**.
2. Connect the actions your assistant needs. For booking, configure Cal.com and select an appointment type. Review required
   caller details, timezone handling and confirmation instructions.
3. Open **Phone numbers**, review the available carrier route and number rental charges, and connect a number you control.
4. Check the assistant's **Publish** readiness, resolve any connection or provider errors, and publish the reviewed version.
5. Run an authorized telephone test before directing customer traffic to it.

An assigned number alone does not prove telephone readiness. Carrier setup, credentials, available funds and the selected
assistant version must all pass the checks shown in your workspace. Number rental is separate from conversation usage and
may continue while a number is idle.

## 5. Use the API when needed

You do not need an API key for browser practice. For an integration, open [API keys](https://burki.dev/api-keys) in your
workspace and create a key with the required access. A read-only first request lists your assistants:

```bash
curl "https://api.burki.dev/api/v1/assistants/" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer YOUR_API_KEY"
```

<Warning>
Keep API keys on your server. Never place them in public browser code, share them in chat, or commit them to source control.
</Warning>
